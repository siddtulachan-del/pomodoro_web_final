What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The Pomodoro Technique breaks work into focused intervals separated by restorative pauses. A clear start and a planned rest can make a large task feel more approachable, while regular breaks give your attention room to recover.

How to use this timer

  1. Choose a focus and break preset, or tailor the lengths in Settings.
  2. Write down the task you want to move forward.
  3. Start the timer and give one thing your attention until the phase ends.
  4. Take your break, then return for the next focus round.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a Pomodoro?

A traditional Pomodoro is 25 minutes of focused work followed by a short break. You can change both durations in Settings.

What is the 52/17 method?

The 52/17 method alternates 52 minutes of focused work with a 17-minute break. Choose the 52/17 preset in Settings.

Does this timer work offline?

Yes. After the first visit, the app's static files are cached so the timer works offline.

Is my data private?

Your settings, timer state, and stats stay in this browser's local storage. There are no accounts.

Can I change the focus and break lengths?

Yes. Choose a preset or adjust each duration and the focus rounds per cycle in Settings.

What is a long break?

A long break is a longer rest that follows a chosen number of completed focus rounds. You can set its duration and frequency.
