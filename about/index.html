About Pomodoro Timer

Pomodoro Timer is a free, calm focus timer built around the Pomodoro Technique: work in focused rounds, then rest. You can use the classic 25/5 rhythm or choose 50/10, 52/17 or 90/20, or set your own focus length, short breaks, long breaks and daily goal.

The timer was made to help people focus without accounts, sign-ups or clutter. Your settings, task labels and stats stay in your browser, the app keeps working offline after your first visit, and you can export or import a JSON backup whenever you like.

Pomodoro Timer is independently built and maintained.
