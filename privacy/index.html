Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Overview

Pomodoro Timer is a free focus timer. It is designed to work without accounts and to keep your information on your own device.

Information stored on your device

Your timer settings, timer state, task labels, statistics and theme choice are saved in your browser's local storage so the timer can remember them between visits. This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to any server. If you turn on notifications or sound alerts, they are handled locally by your browser.

Information we do not collect

We do not require accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email address or other personal details in order to use the timer.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info. Third-party vendors may also place and read cookies on your browser. Ads appear only if and when advertising is enabled on this site.

Analytics

We do not currently use analytics. If this changes, this policy will be updated.

Deleting your data

You can remove your data at any time with Settings > Data > Clear data, or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Backups you export as JSON files are saved on your device and are yours to keep or delete.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
