Overview

This is a free Pomodoro timer designed with privacy in mind.

Stored on your device

Your timer settings, timer state, task labels, stats, and theme are saved in your browser's local storage and are not sent to us. Optional browser notifications and sound alerts are handled locally by your browser.

Not collected

We do not have accounts and do not collect names or email addresses.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info. Third-party vendors may also place and read cookies on your browser. Ads appear only if and when advertising is enabled on this site.

Analytics

We do not currently use analytics. If this changes, this policy will be updated.

Deleting your data

Use Settings > Data > Clear data, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Export JSON and Import JSON backups stay on your device.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted here with a new date.
